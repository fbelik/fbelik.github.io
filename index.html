Current and selected work

Research

I develop computational methods and mathematical models for model reduction, scientific computing, and uncertainty quantification.

Model reduction · Julia

Parametric Model Order Reduction

I develop efficient reduced-order methods for parametric systems, including greedy algorithms, proper orthogonal decomposition, and balanced truncation.

Julia package Documentation

With Akil Narayan and Yanlai Chen.

Arterial blood flow modeling · UQ

Blood Flow, Conductivity, and Uncertainty

I model arterial flow, wall motion, and red blood cell behavior to study how blood pressure influences electrical measurements at the wrist. Sensitivity analysis identifies the parameters that drive model predictions.

Publication

With Christel Hohenegger, Henry Crandall, Benjamin Sanchez, Tyler Schuessler, and Braxton Osting.

Scientific computing · Algorithms

Carathéodory Pruning

I design fast, robust QR-type algorithms that replace a positive discrete measure with a sparse representation while preserving target moments.

Preprint Julia package Documentation

With Akil Narayan and Jesse Chan.

Selected Earlier Projects

Undergraduate honors project

Closed Vortices as Self-Avoiding Polygons

I modeled closed-loop vortices on a cubic lattice and used Markov chain Monte Carlo methods to study the energy distribution of longer configurations.

Presentation

Advised by Pavel Bělík and Thomas LoFaro.

2022 MCM/ICM

Carbon Sequestration in Forests

We developed an age-structured model of planting and harvesting to compare how forest-management strategies affect carbon stored in trees and wood products.

Submission

With Sophia Nelson and Abigayle Paulson.

Discrete mathematics

Port-and-Sweep Solitaire Army Problem

I used board-value functions and linear algebra to establish upper bounds and construct minimal configurations for a one-dimensional solitaire army problem.

Background

Advised by Jacob Siehler.

Machine learning · Networks

Health-Related Habits on Twitter

Advised by Louis Yu; with Jeffery Engelhardt.
